# FounderPostAI growth and distribution plan

This note preserves the post-launch traffic plan so it can be resumed after the plugins themselves are improved.

## Recommended order

1. Recruit 10 design partners from WordPress agencies, freelancers, publishers, and SEO consultants. Offer onboarding and temporary Pro access for candid feedback, then turn successful use into three case studies and five attributable testimonials.
2. Prepare the WordPress.org listings before approval: icon, banner, five workflow screenshots, a short setup video, the first 150 characters, FAQ, and changelog. Respond to reviewer questions promptly and ask established users for honest, non-incentivized reviews only after they receive value.
3. Record three focused demos: the title/meta review workflow, safe internal linking, and integrations with Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, and SEOPress. Publish the full videos on YouTube and reuse short clips on social channels.
4. Run weekly personalized outreach to roughly 10 WordPress agencies or consultants, five specialist creators/reviewers, and five WordPress newsletters, podcasts, or publications. Offer a working demo and temporary access without trying to control the review.
5. Launch on Product Hunt after at least one case study and the demo videos are ready. Ask supporters to visit and leave feedback, not to upvote.
6. Participate helpfully in WordPress communities. Answer questions about metadata, internal linking, hallucinated URLs, and review workflows; mention FounderPostAI only where it genuinely solves the problem and community rules allow it.
7. Build an owned audience with an optional monthly email covering WordPress AI SEO field notes, useful guides, releases, and case studies. Do not gate free plugin downloads behind an email address.
8. Add product-led sharing: a respectful review prompt after demonstrated value, an agency/referral program, a public changelog and roadmap, and an easy feature-request workflow.
9. Delay paid acquisition until activation and upgrade conversion are measurable. Then test narrow, high-intent terms and specialist newsletter sponsorships.

## First 30 days

- Week 1: recruit design partners, prepare WordPress.org assets, and script the demos.
- Week 2: publish three demos and begin personalized agency/creator outreach.
- Week 3: publish the first case study and pitch relevant newsletters or podcasts.
- Week 4: coordinate the Product Hunt launch and follow up with every qualified conversation.

## Metrics to watch

- Website visitor to plugin download
- Plugin download to connected site
- Connected site to first analysis
- First analysis to first applied suggestion
- Free user to Pro checkout
- Case studies, attributable testimonials, referring domains, and qualified email subscribers

The immediate target is 10 active design partners, three credible case studies, three useful demo videos, and five independent mentions or reviews.
